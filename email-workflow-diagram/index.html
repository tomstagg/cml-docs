Email & workflow lifecycle (as-built)¶

End-to-end view of the referral workflow states and every automated email, as built by issues #94/#96/#97. Copy, triggers and suppression rules are canonical in docs/email-spec.md; the as-built engineering summary is docs/system-design.md §11. Times are working days (wd) unless marked calendar; the daily scheduler cycle runs at 09:00 Europe/London.

Legend: 🟦 consumer email · 🟨 firm email · 🟥 internal exception to CML · solid arrows = state changes · dashed arrows = emails/automation.

1. Referral → outcome¶

flowchart TD
    A["Consumer action on results:<br/>Select (Proceed) or Callback request"] --> R(["State: referred"])

    A -.-> C1["🟦 Consumer confirmation<br/>C-01 callback / C-07 select — immediate"]
    A -.-> F1["🟨 Firm referral<br/>F-01 callback / F-03 select — immediate,<br/>from CML, reply-to = consumer"]

    R -.-> F2["🟨 Outcome request<br/>F-02 callback: 3 wd / F-04 select: 10 wd<br/>one-click reporting links"]
    R -.-> C2["🟦 Consumer contact check<br/>C-02 callback: 4 wd / C-08 select: 5 wd<br/>(+ one reminder after 5 calendar days)"]

    F2 -.->|"no answer: reminder at +5 wd,<br/>then exception at +5 wd more"| EXC1["🟥 Exception to CML<br/>automation ends for this action"]

    R -->|"Instruction accepted"| IA(["State: instruction_accepted"])
    R -->|"Unable to act<br/>(Non-Instruction Declaration)"| UA(["State: unable_to_act"])
    R -->|"Unable to contact<br/>(two attempts — callback only)"| UC(["State: unable_to_contact"])
    R -->|"Not instructed /<br/>not expected to proceed"| NI(["State: not_instructed"])

    IA -.-> C5["🟦 C-05 Firm instructed<br/>+ CML feedback link<br/>(reminder after 14 calendar days)"]
    UA -.-> C3["🟦 C-03 / C-09 firm unable to act<br/>back to saved results"]
    UC -.-> C4["🟦 C-04 firm couldn't reach you"]
    NI -.-> C6["🟦 C-06 not instructed<br/>(suppressed if another firm<br/>on the submission was instructed)"]

    NI -->|"Subsequent instruction:<br/>reopen — same transaction id,<br/>history preserved"| IA

2. Open matter → Final Matter Report → billing¶

flowchart TD
    IA(["State: instruction_accepted"]) -.-> F5["🟨 F-05 Matter progress request<br/>10 weeks after acceptance, then monthly<br/>(anniversaries of the instruction date)<br/>suppressed if the period already has an update"]

    F5 -.->|"no answer: reminder at +10 wd,<br/>then exception at +5 wd more"| EXC2["🟥 Exception to CML<br/>automation ends for this period;<br/>an open matter re-enters next period"]

    F5 -->|"one click"| PE(["State: pre_exchange"])
    F5 -->|"one click"| CE(["State: contracts_exchanged"])
    PE --> CE
    PE -.->|"stages are forward-only;<br/>re-confirming the current stage<br/>is a valid periodic report"| F5

    F5 -->|"Matter completed / aborted —<br/>continues into the Final Matter Report<br/>on the same page, never a separate email"| FMR["Final Matter Report<br/>outcome (pre-populated, amendable)<br/>completion / closure date<br/>final legal fee or confirmed none<br/>price comparison vs CML estimate<br/>+ reason when changed"]
    CE --> FMR

    FMR --> DONE(["State: completed / aborted"])
    DONE -.-> BILL["Transaction marked billing-eligible<br/>(billing_eligible_at + full audit detail)"]
    DONE -.-> DEF["🟦 C-10 / C-11 review emails<br/>DEFERRED (D-3) — trigger recorded,<br/>nothing sent"]

3. Reminder & exception pattern (applies to every firm reporting action)¶

flowchart LR
    E["🟨 Reporting email sent"] -->|"unanswered<br/>F-02/F-04: +5 wd · F-05: +10 wd"| REM["🟨 One reminder"]
    REM -->|"unanswered +5 wd"| X["🟥 One exception to CML_EXCEPTION_EMAIL<br/>manual review — no further automation<br/>for this action"]
    E -->|"reported via any channel"| S(["Suppressed — workflow ends quietly"])
    REM -->|"reported via any channel"| S

Every send is logged once per (transaction, template) to email_events (sent/failed at API level) — this makes the daily cycle idempotent and feeds the admin email log at /admin/email-log.
